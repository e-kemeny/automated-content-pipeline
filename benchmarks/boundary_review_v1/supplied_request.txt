KEY FINDINGS:
1. Uniform extension is not reliably beneficial.
2. Event starts and resolutions matter.
3. Victory/payoff screens should be preserved.
4. Avoid including unrelated game transitions.
5. Preserve original boundaries when already good.
6. Extra dead time can reduce pacing.
7. Speech/OCR timestamps do not automatically
   establish exact gameplay event times.
8. Some input footage was previously edited.
9. This is one recording and five candidate
   families, not a representative dataset.
10. Do not claim statistical significance or
    learned generalization from these ratings.
